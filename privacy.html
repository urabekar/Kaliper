Privacy Notice

This notice explains what information Kaliper collects through this website, how it is used and the choices available to you. Kaliper does not sell personal information, share it for marketing purposes or use it for advertising.

Information we collect

When you contact us through the website form or by email, we receive the information you provide: your name, email address, organization and the details of your inquiry. The form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party service that forwards your message to us by email and sends you a confirmation copy. FormSubmit may present a Google reCAPTCHA check to prevent spam. We do not maintain a separate marketing database.

How we use information

We use the information you provide solely to evaluate and respond to your inquiry. We do not add contacts to marketing lists or automated email sequences.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not use analytics. Fonts and images are served from this website and are not loaded from third parties. If analytics are introduced, this notice will be updated before they take effect.

Disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information. We disclose it only where necessary to respond to your inquiry or where required by law.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to confirm what information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. Requests may be sent to hello@kaliperco.com.
